NRL Coaching Career - GitHub Pages copy

1. Create a free account at github.com, then click New repository. Name it (e.g. nrl-coaching-career) and set it to Public.
2. Click "uploading an existing file", then drag in EVERYTHING from this folder: index.html, preview.png, the logos folder and .nojekyll. Commit.
3. Go to Settings > Pages. Under "Build and deployment" choose "Deploy from a branch", branch "main", folder "/ (root)". Save.
4. After a minute your site is at https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO/
5. Open index.html on GitHub (pencil icon) and replace both YOUR-USERNAME and YOUR-REPO in the og:image line near the top with your real names. This makes Discord show your preview image.
6. Optional custom domain: Settings > Pages > Custom domain, then follow GitHub's DNS instructions at your domain seller.

This copy is read-only. To update results, news or streams, send the new details back to Claude and upload the new index.html over the old one.
